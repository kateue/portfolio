About

I'm a developer drawn to the places where language models meet real product problems. My background spans full-stack development, AI strategy, and data analysis. I'm most interested in the gap between what language models can do and what people actually need.

I care about building things that are actually useful, not just technically impressive. That means understanding the problem deeply before reaching for a model, and knowing when a simpler solution is the right one.

I also have a growing interest in contributing to psychology research through the intersection of technology, research, and advocacy.

When I'm not in front of a keyboard, I'm probably outside.
